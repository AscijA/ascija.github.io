About

I’m Maid Ascic, a developer focused on building useful, delightful digital products. I work closely with founders and small teams to ship websites and mobile apps that feel fast and intuitive.

With a strong background in C#, .NET and React, I enjoy turning complex ideas into simple, elegant solutions. My work spans from lightweight landing pages and portfolios to full-featured applications with authentication, databases, and real-time functionality.

Beyond coding, I care deeply about user experience, accessibility, and performance. Every project I take on is guided by the goal of creating software that not only works, but also feels great to use. I’m constantly learning new tools and techniques to keep improving my craft and delivering value to clients.
